FRIENDLITRONICS AMP — install (macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon + Intel)
========================================================================

Two copies of the same plugin are in this folder. Most people want the VST3:

  Friendlitronics Amp.vst3        VST3 — Ableton, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase, ...
  Friendlitronics Amp.component   Audio Unit — Logic, GarageBand

INSTALL
-------
1. In Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste:

       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

   Drag "Friendlitronics Amp.vst3" into that folder.
   (For the Audio Unit, use ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components instead.)

2. Restart your DAW. In Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan.

3. Find it under Plug-Ins > Friendlitronics > Friendlitronics Amp.

That's it. This build is signed and notarised by Apple, so there are no
security warnings and nothing to approve.

FIRST THINGS TO TRY
-------------------
- The PRESET menu at the top right is grouped: FACTORY RIGS are whole setups,
  FACTORY PEDALBOARDS are pedal-only and leave the amp alone.
- The PEDALS tab has a chain strip along the top. Drag the tiles to reorder,
  including the AMP tile — anything left of it feeds the amp input, anything
  right of it runs after the speaker cabinet. Click a tile to switch it on.
- SAVE stores your own presets, and asks whether to save everything, just the
  amp, or just the pedalboard.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Plugin doesn't appear: quit the DAW completely, reopen, rescan. In Ableton
check that "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" is switched on.

Audio Unit doesn't appear in Logic: run this in Terminal, then reopen Logic:

    killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

macOS says the plugin is damaged or from an unidentified developer: that
should not happen with a release build — it means the file was altered in
transit, or you have an older unsigned copy. Delete it, download the release
again, and do not unzip with a third-party archiver.
